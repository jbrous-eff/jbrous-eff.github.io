Reading it: the dashed line is a per-position regression of 2025 production percentile on salary percentile. Distance from the line, not the corner you sit in, is the over/underpaid signal. Slopes are all well below 1 (WR: 0.33, S: 0.51, QB: 0.67) because pay reflects past seasons and projection while production is one noisy year.
Metrics (60% 2025 / 40% 2024 within-position percentiles; single season used when only one exists): QB pass+rush EPA · WR rec EPA · RB scrimmage yds · EDGE/IDL pressure blend · CB PD+INT · S tackle/PD/INT blend · OL snap totals (availability placeholder).
Salary data: Over the Cap, Aug 2026. Players missing one season (injury) are judged on the other; missing both seasons means absent, not judged.
