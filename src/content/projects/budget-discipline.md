question: "How do you guarantee an ad program never overshoots its budget without a platform-level spend cap?"
methodology: "All enabled campaigns' daily budget caps are summed and held exactly at a daily ceiling derived from the monthly budget. A scripted weekly health check recomputes the sum on fresh data and flags any breach, status drift, or new campaign. The bulksheet tools that propose keyword changes write no budget or bid rows, so they are budget-neutral by construction."
finding: "With caps summing exactly to the ceiling, any raise requires an equal trim, so overspend is impossible by construction. Underspend accrues to a flex pool with a dated decision checkpoint instead of silently disappearing."
