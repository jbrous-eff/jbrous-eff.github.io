Every qualifying NFL contract, plotted as salary percentile against production percentile within position. Production blends 2025 and 2024 performance 60/40, and the dashed line is a per-position regression showing what the market actually gets for the money. Distance from that line, not which corner a player sits in, is the over/underpaid signal, and the position filter chips let you isolate any part of the market.
