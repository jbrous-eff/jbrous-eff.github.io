question: "Why did week-over-week ad results keep declining every Monday, then heal themselves days later?"
methodology: "I measured the same reporting periods repeatedly at increasing distance from the period close and compared attributed-conversion totals. Delivery metrics (impressions, clicks, spend) proved final immediately; conversion metrics kept attributing for about 9 days, worst for newly launched campaigns."
finding: "Conversions settle 15 to 19% above the day-0 reading over about 9 days. The operating rules that follow: compare weeks only at equal maturity, and build client reporting only from data pulled 10 or more days after month close."
